## Add a component

1. Find a component in the **Components** panel.
2. Drag it onto the canvas.
3. Release it where you want it to appear.

Start with a **Toggle Switch**, a gate, and an **Output**. Add wires once the components are in place.

## Arrange the circuit

Drag a component to move it. A useful starting layout is inputs on the left, gates in the middle, and outputs on the right.

Leave enough space around each component to see its handles and follow its connections.

## Grid and snapping

Open **Settings**, then **Canvas**, to control the grid and **Snap to Grid**.

The grid is a visual guide. Snapping aligns components to grid positions while you move them. Choose the combination that helps you arrange your circuit.

For the next step, see [Wiring and connections](/learn/wiring).
