AND, OR, and NOT operate on binary values: `0` and `1`. Their outputs follow a small set of rules.

## AND gate

AND outputs `1` when both inputs are `1`. Try turning on either input alone, then both together.

## OR gate

OR outputs `1` when at least one input is `1`. Compare its output with AND when only one switch is on.

## NOT gate

NOT has one input and one output. It inverts the input: `0` becomes `1`, and `1` becomes `0`.

<TruthTable headers={["Input", "Output"]} rows={[[0, 1], [1, 0]]} />

## Combine gates

A gate's output can become another gate's input. Placing NOT after AND gives NAND; placing NOT after OR gives NOR.

Continue with [NAND and NOR gates](/learn/nand-nor-gates) to compare these inverted outputs.
