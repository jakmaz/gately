## Include a carry

A full adder has three inputs: **A**, **B**, and **Carry In**. It produces **Sum** and **Carry Out**.

The extra input carries a result from the previous bit position when adding multi-bit numbers.

## Try the circuit

Turn on one input, then two, then all three. With all three inputs set to `1`, Sum and Carry Out are both `1`, giving the binary result `11`.

## Combine two half adders

The first half adder adds A and B. The second adds that sum to Carry In. An OR gate combines their carry outputs.

- **Sum:** `A XOR B XOR Carry In`
- **Carry Out:** `(A AND B) OR (Carry In AND (A XOR B))`

This uses two XOR gates, two AND gates, and one OR gate.

## Add more bits

Connect Carry Out from one full adder to Carry In on the next bit position. This arrangement is called a **ripple-carry adder**.

Each stage adds one pair of bits and passes its carry to the next stage.
